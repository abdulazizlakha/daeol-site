FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about AEO, GEO, AI search optimization and how DAEOL works.

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of making your content answer-focused so AI-powered search tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google Gemini can understand and reference it accurately. It focuses on clear structure, direct responses to real questions, and well-supported brand information.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) improves your content’s context, structure and brand information consistency so generative AI tools can interpret your business accurately.

How is GEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO helps pages rank in search results. GEO helps AI tools understand and represent your brand inside the answers they write. They complement each other, and we run them together.

Can DAEOL guarantee that ChatGPT or another AI tool will mention my business?

No. Nobody controls what an AI platform says, and no one can honestly guarantee a mention. We improve the signals these tools rely on and report what changes.

How long does AEO and GEO take to show results?

Usually a few months, not days. Timing depends on your starting point and competition, which is why Basic Answer has a 3-month minimum.

Do I need SEO before investing in GEO?

Solid technical SEO makes your content easier for both search engines and AI tools to find and understand. We check your foundations in the audit and fix gaps as part of the work.
